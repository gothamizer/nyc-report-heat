How The Hot 100 works

Ranks NYC government reports by how often the exact document is shared in public.

1 Sources tracked

—: city and state watchdogs, the City Council, agency report libraries, NYC Rules, and the Government Publications Portal. Links that circulate before a listing surfaces them are added automatically. The full list is in the footer.

2 Where we count pickup

Every link to a tracked report shared on:

  • Bluesky: posts, weighted by likes/reposts/replies
  • Reddit: posts, weighted by score/comments
  • Hacker News: stories, weighted by points/comments
  • NYC newsrooms: Gothamist, THE CITY, City & State, NY Post, NYT Metro, amNY, City Limits, PIX11, and more

An article counts when it links the document or names a tracked report by its exact title.

3 Ranking

News articles linking the exact URL count most, then articles naming the title, then social posts weighted by engagement, then filename pickups.

How heat is measured

Heat counts public pickups of the exact document: news articles linking the URL weigh most, then articles naming the report's exact title, then social posts weighted by engagement, then filename pickups.

Every counted pickup is a real post or article carrying the exact link, the document's filename, or its exact title alongside the publishing body's name.

Windows

Three rolling windows (today, 7d, and 30d) are recomputed across the whole inventory on every run. A document stays tracked after it scrolls off a source's recent page.
